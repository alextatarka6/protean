Model facts
Reward shaping

Win or loss (±100) swamps the smaller HP, status, and KO terms — same balance the metamon paper uses in Appendix E.1.

PPO hyperparameters

Clip ε 0.2 · γ/λ 0.999/0.95 · LR 1e-4 · minibatch 256 · KL β 0.1 · 3 frozen BC opponents + 1 self-play copy, resynced every 20 episodes.
